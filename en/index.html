The meaning of Pascha

Why this feast matters

✝️

The victory of life

Pascha is not simply an ancient tradition. It proclaims that Christ has risen and that death no longer has the last word.

🕯️

An open invitation

Whether you are Orthodox, a Christian of another tradition, or simply curious, you are welcome. Come with your questions and with an open heart.

⛪

The Orthodox Paschal service

The night service includes the Midnight Office, the procession around the church, Paschal Matins and the Divine Liturgy.
